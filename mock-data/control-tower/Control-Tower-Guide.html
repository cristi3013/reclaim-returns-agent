O2C Control Tower Agent

Find where money leaks out of order-to-cash, and hand each leak to the agent that fixes it.

Money leaks quietly from the order-to-cash flow: goods shipped but never invoiced, invoices that wait weeks for a proof of delivery, orders on a block nobody works on, customers who pay late. Each of these is visible in SAP, but in a different screen, and they usually surface at month-end, if at all.

This agent walks every order's document chain against the reference process, measures values and ageing, answers management questions such as “why is DSO up for Norway?” or “what blocks the close?”, writes a close-readiness memo, and routes every finding to the agent that fixes it. It only reads: it never changes SAP.

Input“Run the snapshot”, or a question naming a country, customer or order
OutputKPIs per currency, ranked findings with an L4 step and a route, a close-readiness memo, routing notes

1 · Order-to-Cash in five minutesFrom a customer's question to money in the bank

Order-to-Cash (O2C) is everything a company does between a customer asking for something and the money arriving. SAP S/4HANA records every step as a document (inquiry, quotation, order, delivery, invoice…) that points back to the one before it. That chain is the document flow: from any invoice you can walk back to the order it came from. Follow one example through the stages. The Control Tower works at the last one, and reads all the others.

  1. 1
    Pre-sales

    The customer asks: what would it cost, can you deliver? We record the question and send an offer.

    “What would 20 drums cost, delivered by 15 October?” → inquiry, then quotation

  2. 2
    Sales order reads

    The customer orders. SAP checks price, credit and availability.

    Order 1876: 40 KG for customer 10057, 10 800 EUR

  3. 3
    Blocks reads

    An order can be held for credit, price or delivery reasons until someone releases it.

    Order 1368 waits on billing block 02

  4. 4
    Delivery & POD reads

    The warehouse ships; the goods leave our stock (goods issue). Some customers must confirm receipt first: the proof of delivery (POD).

    Delivery 80608983 leaves on 30 Sep; the POD is still open

  5. 5
    Billing reads

    We invoice what was actually delivered.

    Order 1937 → delivery 80609033 → invoice 90000455

  6. 6
    Returns & credit reads

    If something is wrong, the customer sends goods back or gets money back.

    A return with no credit note after a week is a finding

  7. 7
    Cash reads

    We match incoming payments to open invoices and chase late payers.

    Customer 10020 owes 50 000 EUR, past due

  8. 8
    Reporting & close this agent

    We report what is stuck, late or leaking, and decide whether the month can close.

    “Not ready: 31 deliveries with goods issue in September are unbilled for more than 14 days.”

2 · The process this agent must coverL3 processes and L4 steps

The hackathon uses SAP's O2C process hierarchy: L1 Order to Cash → L2 stage → L3 process → L4 step, each with an ID. The Control Tower owns the reporting steps, and it checks the steps of every other stage. Every finding it reports names one L4 ID, the same IDs the other teams use, so a finding lands with the right team in the right words. "Who acts" is who fixes the finding: this agent never does.

3 · Build itSAP APIs and BTP services

Your agent talks to SAP through the standard, released OData APIs of S/4HANA, the same data a user sees in the screens. Build it with any stack you like: your laptop, or SAP BTP with a trial account. Every call below was tested on DS4. If your team uses the hackathon MCP server, the tool that wraps each call is named in the last column.

Connecting to SAP DS4

TopicHow
Base URLhttps://<ds4-host>:<port>/sap/opu/odata/sap/<SERVICE>/. Host, port and a technical user per team come from the hackathon lead.
ClientAdd sap-client=100 to every request (query parameter).
AuthenticationBasic authentication with the team's technical user.
FormatOData V2. Add $format=json (or header Accept: application/json); results are in d.results (lists) or d (one record).
Reading$filter, $select, $expand, $top, $skip, $orderby as in any OData V2 service. Strings in single quotes: SoldToParty eq '10044'.
PagingAsk for a page ($top=100) and the next one ($skip=100) until a page comes back short. A list that returns exactly its $top is not complete.
WritingNever. This agent sends GET requests only. No CSRF token, no POST, PATCH or DELETE, no MCP write tool.
DatesOData V2 dates are milliseconds since 1970 in UTC: "/Date(1790726400000)/" = 30 Sep 2026 (00:00 UTC). In a filter: ActualGoodsMovementDate le datetime'2026-09-28T00:00:00'.
NumbersQuantities and amounts are strings: "TotalNetAmount": "10800.00". Always keep the currency next to the amount.
NetworkDS4 is on the client network. From your laptop: VPN. From BTP: the Connectivity service + SAP Cloud Connector, with a Destination pointing at DS4. Ask the hackathon lead; one Cloud Connector serves all teams.
ErrorsSAP returns error.message.value with a readable text. A section that fails (no authorisation, unknown field) goes into the memo as not read; the rest of the run continues.

Things DS4 does that you would not expect

  • Lists stop at their row cap. With $top=100 the unbilled list returns exactly 100 rows, oldest first, so it stops partway through 25 Sep 2026 and every later delivery is cut off; the full list has 178. The blocked-order list is sorted by value, so small team orders (1369, 1373, 1833) fall off at 100; the full list has 144. Page, or narrow by customer or date, and say so in the memo.
  • One delivery, two lists. All 80 deliveries waiting for POD are also on the unbilled list: a POD-relevant delivery is not invoiced before its POD. Count it once, cause POD, route to the POD Chaser.
  • The conformance check has no grace period. Order 1876 was delivered on 30 Sep 2026 and is flagged at 4.1.1 “delivered but not billed” on 1 Oct. One day is not a leak; apply your own grace (3 days is a good start).
  • Delivery headers carry no value. Take NetAmount from the billing due list (F0798). On DS4 it lists POD-pending deliveries too (e.g. 80609014, 3 400 EUR, POD open), so you can value them; it does not mean they should be billed before the POD.
  • Two currencies. Company code YDE1 (sales org YSOD) books in EUR, YRO1 (YSOR) in RON. Report per currency; never add them. A customer can owe in both (10021: 318 508.40 EUR and 5 RON).
  • Old leftovers. 34 unbilled deliveries date from 2019–2025 (oldest 80000033, goods issue 13 Mar 2019), two blocked orders are years old (143, 1120). Report them as legacy, apart from this month's close. Legacy does not mean ownerless: 29 of the 34 old deliveries are the teams' own Billing due-list items in the team data sheet (e.g. T01: 80000170, 80000244, 80000313, 80000443), so label them with that team.
  • No customer in Norway. No business partner on DS4 has a Norwegian address. Customers in the findings are in DE, RO, US, CH and JP; 46, 51 and 7800000096 have no address. Your agent must say so, not invent an answer.
  • Exact DSO is not in O2C data. It needs revenue per period from finance. Explain DSO through its drivers: overdue receivables, unbilled and POD-pending value, billing blocks.
  • The leakage scan's blocked, unbilled and POD sections ignore companyCode; only the overdue section uses it. Call it once per company code, or read the lists yourself.
  • The billing due list service answers HTTP 500 to $orderby: sort locally.

The read-only rule (build it in)

  • Your agent never writes to SAP. No write tool, no POST/PATCH/DELETE, not even with a person's approval: a request to change something (“just release the block”) becomes a routing note to the agent that owns the step.
  • Every finding names the data owner: your team, another team (from the team data sheet), shared hackathon data, or other. A routing note informs; the receiving team acts only on its own documents, behind its own approval.
  • Keep your own log: each run's SAP requests (GET only), the snapshot, the findings and the notes sent. The jury will ask to see it.
  • Every number in an answer or memo comes from a SAP read of that run. A language model may phrase the answer; it never changes a figure, a severity or a route.

4 · SAP words, our data and your mock dataWhat you need to know, nothing more

SAP wordPlain meaning
Goods issue (PGI)The posting that the goods left our warehouse. From then on the delivery should be invoiced.
Proof of delivery (POD)The customer's confirmation that the goods arrived. For POD-relevant customers the invoice waits for it.
Billing due listEverything ready to be invoiced (VF04, Fiori Create Billing Documents), with its value.
Unbilled shipped valueGoods that left the warehouse but carry no invoice yet: revenue not on the books.
BlockA hold on an order: billing, delivery or credit. Block ageing = how long it has been held.
Overdue / open itemAn invoice not yet paid (open item). Overdue: its net due date has passed.
DSODays sales outstanding: how many days of sales are still unpaid. Rises with overdue invoices and with invoices sent late.
Document flowThe chain order → delivery → invoice (→ payment) SAP keeps for every order.
ConformanceDoes an order's document flow follow the reference process? A deviation names the L4 step where it does not.
Close readinessCan the month be closed: is all shipped revenue invoiced, is anything material stuck?
Company codeThe legal entity that books the money: YDE1 (EUR) and YRO1 (RON) on DS4.

Logging on

  1. SAP GUI: open SAP Logon, double-click DS4, enter Client 100, your user and password, Language EN.
  2. Start a screen by typing its transaction code in the command field (top left), e.g. /nVA03, then Enter. /n leaves the current screen first; F3 goes back; F4 in a field searches values; F8 runs a report.
  3. Fiori: open the launchpad URL from the hackathon lead (it ends in /sap/bc/ui2/flp?sap-client=100), log on with the same user, and use the search at the top to find an app by name.
  4. Documents show without leading zeros on screen (80609071); dates show as 30.09.2026. APIs use 0080609071 where the field is padded, and 2026-09-30.

5 · Business value calculatorWhat is this agent worth?

Every value is an editable assumption for a mid-sized business unit, not a measurement: our sandbox holds test data. Replace them with the numbers of the business you pitch to. The calculation is deliberately simple so anyone can check it.

Value per year –
Hours saved per year–
Labour value per year–
People freed (FTE)–
Cash released once–
Financing gain per year–
Cost of errors avoided per year–

hours saved / year = findings per month × (minutes today − minutes with agent) ÷ 60 × 12 labour value / year = hours saved × loaded cost per hour FTE freed = hours saved ÷ 1 600 productive hours per person cash released (once) = findings per month × value per finding × days sooner ÷ 30 financing gain / yr = cash released × cost of capital value per year = labour value + financing gain

Cash released is working capital: a delivery invoiced fifteen days sooner is paid fifteen days sooner, so that much less money is tied up, permanently. The financing gain is what that money is worth per year.

Gains that are real but not priced here

  • Leakage found in days, not at month-end: a cleaner, faster close.
  • One prioritised list across every O2C stage, in the same L4 language every team uses.
  • A close-readiness memo instead of spreadsheet hunting, and honest answers when SAP holds none.
  • Revenue that would never have been invoiced at all (legacy deliveries) is found and decided on.

6 · Checks in SAPMake sure the agent is doing the right thing

Do each check by hand before you trust the agent: it shows you what the agent should find. Do it again after a run: the agent's numbers must match SAP's screens. Each check has the SAP GUI route (transaction code) and the Fiori route (browser app). They show the same data. Check 3

Deliveries waiting for POD

The third driver: invoices that wait for the customer's proof of delivery.

  1. /nSE16N, Enter. Table: LIKP (delivery header), Enter.
  2. Selection: WBSTK (goods movement status) = C; PDSTK (POD status) = A or B (multiple selection); WADAT_IST (actual goods issue date) ≤ 28.09.2026.
  3. Press F8. Count the rows; sort by WADAT_IST.
  4. No SE16N authorisation? Open a single delivery in VL03N and choose Environment → Document flow: no invoice after the delivery, and no POD.

DS4 offers participants no list app for open PODs. Use SE16N or the API (section 3).

You should see

80 deliveries on 1 Oct 2026, oldest 80000271 (2021). Team T01's 80609014 has goods issue 18.09.2026 and POD status A. The agent's POD list must match, and none of them may be counted again as a billing finding.
